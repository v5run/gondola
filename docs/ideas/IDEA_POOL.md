# Idea pool: new angles and variations

November 6, 2026. Generated by the assistant in response to the user's request for more ideas and a better process. These are exploratory hypotheses, not user selections, verified novelty, or implemented features. [Leading ideas](LEADING_IDEAS.md) remain the current shortlist. [The playbook](IDEATION_PLAYBOOK.md) contains reusable generation/challenge/research prompts.

## P01 - Shape incoming demand around the kitchen bottleneck

**Origin:** Develop the user's dynamic-pricing/deals suggestion by connecting it to per-station capacity.

**Example:** The fryer is saturated but another station has spare capacity. The agent considers featuring approved items that use the available station, changing a first-party recommendation, or withholding an otherwise appealing promotion that would worsen the queue. The objective includes contribution, service promises, and incentive cost.

**Agent role:** Interpret operating context and merchant constraints, propose supported interventions, and evaluate their outcomes. Optimizers/checks calculate resource requirements and enforce limits. Customer response is unknown and must be learned or explicitly modeled; an LLM cannot declare an offer profitable from a simulated click.

**Evidence:** [Open's primary description](https://www.getopen.com/blog/restaurant-ai-decision-engine) already includes kitchen load/backlog/stock and autonomous marketing choices. Broad kitchen-aware marketing is not a novelty claim. Station-specific intervention quality and added benefit remain untested.

**Sponsor-first update:** DoorDash also has native automated Smart Campaigns; exact per-station integration and our campaign API entitlement are unverified. See [DoorDash fit and access](DOORDASH_FIT_AND_ACCESS.md) before proposing an adapter or claiming this as a missing DoorDash feature.

**First test:** Small approved menu, two resource constraints, fixed budget and accepted commitments; compare station-aware selection with a strong margin/stock-aware rule. Vary modeled customer response and include a case where no promotion is best. A real demand/contribution effect requires an appropriate real experiment. Initial first-party preview avoids assuming marketplace promotion permissions.

**Status:** User-favored on November 6 and promoted to the leading index. [Detailed assessment](DEMAND_STEERING.md) records the Open adoption check, integrated scheduling/steering proposal, and first comparison. No campaign sent, pricing changed, or full build selected.

## P02 - Coordinate cooking and truthful platform readiness estimates

**Origin:** Challenge the assumption that courier arrival is an external countdown independent of our decisions.

**Example:** A kitchen estimate influences courier dispatch; a courier update then changes the kitchen's scheduling decision. Repeatedly chasing these estimates can produce a poor plan. Investigate a joint policy for unstarted work and truthful readiness updates that respects original commitments and stale/missing observations.

**Evidence:** [Uber's official guide](https://developer.uber.com/docs/eats/guides/order-integration) says courier dispatch uses predicted prep time and integrations can set/update ready times. This proves a coupling, not the proposed improvement or our write access. Existing Captain dispatch-driven sequencing is adjacent competition; do not claim exclusivity.

**First test:** A disclosed synthetic dispatcher that responds to prep estimates, uncertain arrival revisions, and a station slowdown. Compare with ETA-only scheduling and a sensible preparation-estimate baseline. Track lateness, holding, courier wait, and schedule churn; changing quoted times must not erase lateness or silently change customer promises. Actual platform behavior needs calibration and approved access.

**Status:** Extension of the [kitchen scheduler](ORDER_SCHEDULING.md), not another independent product. Controller authority never includes choosing or directing marketplace couriers.

## P03 - Learn the owner's soft tradeoffs through examples

**Origin:** The user's FIFO/fairness objection exposes operational priorities that a generic objective may miss.

**Example:** Show two valid responses to a hypothetical rush. The owner chooses, explains a preference, or says the information is insufficient. The agent builds an editable account of soft priorities, then applies them to unseen cases and asks when uncertainty matters. Maximum waits, existing promises, budgets, and other hard rules remain explicit.

**Evidence:** Pairwise preference elicitation is established; [1000minds' method description](https://www.1000minds.com/paprika) is an example of that prior art, not a method we have implemented. General personalized agents and merchant onboarding also overlap. The proposed novelty is not preference learning itself.

**First test:** Several bounded choices followed by held-out tradeoffs; measure agreement with subsequent owner decisions, questions required, uncertainty, and hard-rule violations. Include conflicting/inconsistent responses. Do not infer true priorities from arbitrary synthetic examples or historical actions that may reflect missing information.

**Status:** Alternative way to configure the earlier owner-mandate feature, not an extra whole product. Human judgment is a useful input, not a deficiency to remove.

## P04 - Distinguish demand changes from effects of the agent's own actions

**Example:** A promotion, channel pause, and staffing change occur during the same period. Subsequent sales are partly shaped by those actions. The agent maintains an intervention history, investigates alternative explanations for a miss, and proposes an identifiable comparison instead of simply learning that underlying demand changed.

**First test:** Controlled fixtures with known interventions and hidden causes; compare naive updates with an intervention-aware process. The agent must admit when causes cannot be distinguished. Simulated known truth can validate the supported cases, not causal claims about a real merchant.

**Status:** Variant of the existing policy/scientific-learning direction. Ordinary stockout-censored forecasting is known baseline work and is not being reintroduced as a new invention.

## Maps as a potential input

[Places Insights](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/placesinsights/overview) documents POI counts/density and access signup. [Population Dynamics Insights](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/population-dynamics-insights/overview) describes BigQuery embeddings incorporating aggregated search, busyness visitation, POI and environmental patterns. These descriptions do not establish a live restaurant-level footfall feed, current merchant demand, available project access/credits, or incremental predictive value. No dataset subscription, Maps request, or BigQuery query was run. The useful first question is which decision an available signal would improve beyond weather/events/POS history.
