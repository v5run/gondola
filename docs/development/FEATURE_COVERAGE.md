## Largest remaining gap against the original baseline

The discussed autonomous loop was: gather merchant sales/customer data plus local signals, predict demand, update stock/staff plans, observe misses, investigate them and improve future plans. The repo has parts of forecasting/planning on synthetic data and the new operational replay, but that full continuously operating loop is not connected or verified.

The next scope decision should identify which missing outcome to demonstrate. Do not describe current parameter tuning as arbitrary policy invention, ordinary completion updates as causal demand learning, or local replay as a live always-on merchant agent.

Measured (synthetic, full days, default settings): see the commit report. On normal days the queue meets more promises than FIFO with somewhat less holding; with the earlier default JIT gate (15%) it met **fewer** promises than FIFO (2026-11-04: 76.9% vs 93.4%) while halving holding. The 50% default was chosen after measuring 2026-11-03/04 on the principle that holding reductions must not cost promises (curve: 15/30/50/never -> 76.9/87.6/96.7/96.7% met, hold 5.8/6.6/7.8/8.7 min on 11-04). Under overload the queue alone is **worse** than FIFO on promises met (11-05: 65.5% vs 70.1%); the full Gondola arm's gains on busy nights come mostly from admission control (declines and quotes), which are counted.

Remaining gaps: settings changes apply to the whole replayed day (not from the change time) and earlier ledger events keep their first-written values; cold start ~8 s (Future nowcast per 15-min bin); no offer demand response; pause/busy treated as lost orders; agent LLM quality unverified (no live call in this pass); Present's order.received duplicates the POS adapter's event per order (different keys/sources; the ledger summary prefers Present's).
