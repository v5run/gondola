# Gondola QA report (independent)

Worked only in `/home/claude/qa-copy`. My scripts are in `/home/claude/qa-scripts/` (`ask_qs.py`, `guard_t.py`, `edge.py`, `books_eval.py`, `multi.sh`). Ground truth came from `data/raw/*.csv` (pandas) and `data/truth/`.

## Summary verdict

**OK for a scripted demo on the single happy path. Not safe for a live demo that strays from it, and not credible as a product yet.**
- The demo close (`--date 2026-11-05 --force`, first run) works and its numbers are right.
- Running the same documented command a second time drops the headline DoorDash finding and sends a different SMS.
- Every other close date fails or degrades.
- The Ask router answers many ordinary questions with confident wrong numbers.
- Payout "verification" quietly uses reports that have not arrived yet.
- The guardrail is never called in the agent code, and it lets wrong numbers through.
- Much of the detection accuracy is circular: the rules mirror how the generator injects anomalies.
